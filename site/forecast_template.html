Wind flow

NOAA GFS, every 6 hours for 7 days, 1° grid. Tap the map to read speed and direction.

Sea-level pressure (average of models)

Mean sea-level pressure averaged across the models available at each time (listed under the slider), every 6 hours. Isobars every 2 hPa; L marks a low-pressure centre, H a high. "Spread" shows how much the models disagree: large spread around a low means its position or depth is uncertain. Tap the map to read values.
